# SOHO Fashion — The City Edit for Mark’s

First interactive concept, October 2026. This is a supplier's collection proposal, not the official Mark’s retail website.

## Preview

Serve `dist/` with a static HTTP server. No dependencies or build step are needed. Main files are `dist/index.html`, `dist/styles.css`, and `dist/app.js`.

## Included

- Responsive editorial homepage in English and Chinese.
- Four clearly labelled concept garments, category filters, bilingual search and sorting.
- Product details, local-device favourites, votes and comments; downloadable shortlist.
- Up to three consumer priorities with local persistence.
- Weekly email mockup and an explicitly non-sending subscription flow.
- Two-page Chinese business questionnaire, generated by `create_questionnaire.py` into `output/pdf/`.

## Scope and next steps

This version validates visual direction and browsing/feedback flows. No product prices, exact fabric composition or performance certifications are invented. Example comments are labelled, not presented as real customer feedback.

Saved selections, votes, preferences and new comments use browser localStorage. They are not shared across devices. Subscription inputs are not stored or transmitted, and no mail is sent. Before store-consumer use, connect persistent feedback storage, moderation and privacy controls, consent-based subscription management and an actual weekly email provider; confirm public versus Mark’s-only product fields. The hosted review site is owner-private, not yet a consumer-facing rollout.

Replace all illustrative items with Sheng's confirmed styles, style codes, fabrics, imagery and selling points. Use her answers on client priorities, customer pain points and store feedback to revise the collection taxonomy, product detail emphasis and feedback prompts.

## Design basis and assets

The existing SOHO Fashion Style3D showroom supplies the collection-first context. Mark’s official site (https://www.marks.com/en.html) was reviewed for broad women/men and practical everyday clothing context. This concept does not reproduce its official visual identity or claim affiliation beyond a collection proposal.

All five garment/editorial images were generated with the built-in image generation tool, not scraped product photographs. Prompts/briefs:

1. `urban-hero.png`: premium city fashion editorial, adult man and woman in navy and cream utility layers, warm limestone Toronto-style city street, subjects on the right and negative space left, natural light, no logos/text.
2. `navy-jacket.png`: clean product photograph of a navy utility/chore jacket, patch pockets, warm neutral studio background, no logo/text.
3. `sand-jacket.png`: clean product photograph of a sand-coloured women's utility jacket, refined boxy silhouette, warm neutral studio background, no logo/text.
4. `ivory-knit.png`: ivory textured rib crewneck knit sweater, premium product photography, square composition, warm off-white seamless background, no branding.
5. `charcoal-trouser.png`: charcoal wide-leg pleated women's trousers, softly structured fabric, premium product photography, warm off-white background, no branding.

Images are concept visuals only. Original generated files are retained outside this checkout. The PDF uses Microsoft YaHei embedded fonts for reliable Chinese rendering.
